FAQ

Straight answers

Will the price change once you start?

You get the price before work starts. If Marc finds something else, he shows you and explains it, and you decide. Nothing extra is added without your OK, in writing by text.

Why is there a deposit?

A deposit holds your time slot and covers the trip out. It is credited to your final bill, so it's not an extra charge.

Can I buy my own parts?

Yes. Choose "I have my own parts" in the form and include the part name or number so Marc can confirm it fits your exact vehicle before the visit. Warranty on customer-supplied parts comes from the parts seller.

Is the work guaranteed?

Marc stands behind his work. Warranty terms depend on the repair and the parts used, and they're written on your quote before work begins, so you know exactly what's covered.

What can't be done in a driveway?

Most diagnostics, brakes, batteries, alternators, starters, belts, suspension, AC and maintenance can be done on site. If a job truly needs a lift or a shop, Marc tells you up front.

How do I pay?

Credit and debit cards, tap-to-pay on Marc's phone, or the secure online links on this page for deposits and invoices.
